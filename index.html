The process

One idea.
Four stages.

  1. 01Design

    Define the idea and prepare the digital form.

  2. 02CNC

    Machine, carve and engrave the chosen material.

  3. 03Finish

    Smooth, colour and refine the surface.

  4. 04Complete

    Inspect and prepare the finished piece.

About E-CUSTOMS

Technology with a craftsman’s eye.

E-CUSTOMS CNC Woodworks is a Kenyan creative manufacturing business focused on custom wooden artwork, personalized designs and precision craftsmanship.

Based in Eldoret, we combine digital design, CNC technology and careful finishing to transform ideas into distinctive wooden pieces.
